The model must expose uncertainty. A single successful demonstration does not establish how to handle every exception; missing business rules require human input.

## Evaluation

Include matching quantities, shortages, missing quantities, changed layouts, malicious instructions embedded in documents, and failed saves. Do not let the agent's self-reported success stand in for independent verification.
